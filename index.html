豪斯工作室自2000年就成立，曾在廣告公司任職3年，自行接案4年，在各大企業擔任美工設計共10年。 每一件作品的完成，無論小到一張名片、海報，或大到會場施工，都是經歷不斷的溝通討論，所以您應該選擇「溝通能力強的」及「能解決問題的」專業人員。
Haous Studio has been around since 2000. We started out with 3 years in an ad agency, followed by 4 years of freelancing, and have since spent over a decade doing graphic design work for major companies. Over the years, we’ve come to understand that every project—whether it’s something as small as a business card or as big as setting up an event space—takes a lot of back-and-forth communication to get just right. That’s why we believe you should work with someone who’s not only creative, but also a great communicator and a problem-solver.
